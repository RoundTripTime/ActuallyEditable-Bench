Illustrative teaching extension of The Window Room; not a client record, supplier quote, site survey, or construction-ready document. Pending professional review.

How to use

Complete this after discovery, not instead of discovery. Read the objectives back to the client in plain language. Revisit the brief when scope changes rather than quietly changing its meaning in a presentation. Preserve the agreed brief revision when discussing new needs; review effects on scope separately.

  • Extract needs and constraints from the questionnaire.
  • Check each proposed deliverable against the objectives.
  • Confirm unresolved items and review the brief before starting the next phase.

Review before use

  • Avoid vague aims such as “make it better” without explaining use.
  • Do not hide exclusions in notes that nobody reviews.
  • Do not treat a signed-off aesthetic direction as approval of dimensions or cost.

Replace illustrative information with verified project records. No approval, legal agreement, or construction readiness is represented. Keep completed client documents in your own secure system.
