Tool:

Purpose:

Audience/ User:

Training Log

To record all training completed by site study staff members that is not otherwise documented by a training completion certificate

Study coordinators, principal investigators, other site staff, clinical monitor

Details:

This tracking should provide a comprehensive list of all training completed by site study staff that is not documented by other written means, such as a completion certificate. It is required for both observational and interventional clinical research studies. log
