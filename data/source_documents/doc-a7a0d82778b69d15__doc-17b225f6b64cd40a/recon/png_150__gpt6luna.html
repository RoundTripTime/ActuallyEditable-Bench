How to use

Send the blank questionnaire before discovery, then review it together. Invite short answers and allow “not sure yet”; a missing answer is a follow-up task, not permission to assume. Store completed forms in your own secure project system, not on this website. Keep original answers and the agreed interpretation distinguishable; confirm who consolidates feedback.

  • Send the questions with a short explanation of how answers will be used.
  • Discuss conflicting priorities in discovery; record questions rather than resolving them silently.
  • Transfer agreed needs into the brief and assign follow-up information to an owner.

Review before use

  • Do not treat an inspiration image as a specification.
  • Do not convert an aspirational budget or date into a commitment.
  • Do not collect unnecessary sensitive household information.

Replace illustrative information with verified project records. No approval, legal agreement, or construction readiness is represented. Keep completed client documents in your own secure system.
